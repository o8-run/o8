# Pro · Lifetime

Pro · Lifetime is the one-time purchase cohort. Its members keep their included
benefits for life. The future monthly plan is called Pro and is separate from
Pro · Lifetime.

The cohort was previously called Founding Operators. The new name describes the
lifetime license without implying a role in the company.

Existing lifetime licenses continue to work and keep every benefit, including
managed inference, remote access, and early access to experimental features.
The name change does not convert a lifetime license into a monthly subscription.

The app uses Pro · Lifetime in plan labels, settings, account copy, and the
lifetime badge tooltip. The badge keeps each member's existing serial number.

See [the plan overview](../README.md#free-and-open-source) and [o8 Pro](https://o8.run/pro).
