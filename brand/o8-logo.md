# o8 — brand logo (retired 2026-11-12)

> **Retired.** The o8 logo is now the Ring. See [docs/design/BRAND.md](../docs/design/BRAND.md). Kept for history only.

The official o8 app/brand mark. **Agents: use this as the o8 logo.** It replaces the
older amber/teal glowing icon.

## The mark
The **o8 monogram** — a lowercase `o` whose stroke flows in ONE continuous ribbon and
twists into the digit `8` (a connected, infinity-like loop; keep this twist). It sits
under a **glass tile**:

- **Cool blue rim light** along the top edge (`#8FB4FF` periwinkle)
- **Warm ember glow** pooling faintly at the base
- **One punchy ember-orange tick** on the upper arc of the 8 — the single pop of color (`#FF5A1F`)
- Charcoal-ink mark (`#23262E`) on warm cream/beige glass

Design language: crisp, clean, premium — glass with restraint, one orange accent per frame.
